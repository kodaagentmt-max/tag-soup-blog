Goose Gumbo

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Dark, rich goose gumbo with the giblets and leftover goose meat.

What You'll Need

1 goose carcass, 1 lb andouille sausage, 1 cup gumbo file, 1 can diced tomatoes, 3 cups rice, 2 onions, 3 celery stalks, garlic, thyme, bay leaves, chicken broth

Roast carcass at 400°F for 30 min. Sauté vegetables, add tomatoes and broth, simmer with carcass 2 hours. Strain, return to pot, add sausage and goose meat, thicken with roux or file. Serve over rice.

Pro Tips

Gumbo file thickens and adds authentic flavor — stir in after the boil.
