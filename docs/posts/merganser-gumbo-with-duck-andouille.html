Merganser Gumbo with Duck Andouille

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Merganser duck and homemade andouille sausage in a dark roux gumbo.

What You'll Need

1 merganser duck, 1 lb andouille, 3/4 cup flour, 3/4 cup oil (for roux), 1 large onion, 1 bell pepper, 3 celery stalks, 6 cups duck or chicken stock, 2 tbsp gumbo file, rice

Make dark roux: heat oil, whisk in flour, cook stirring constantly until dark brown. Add Trinity (onion, pepper, celery), cook 10 min. Add stock, duck and andouille, simmer 2 hours. Stir in file, serve over rice.

Pro Tips

The roux is the heart of the gumbo — don't rush it.
