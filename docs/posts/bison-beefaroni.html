Bison

Bison Beefaroni

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Cylindrical pasta in a rich bison and tomato meat sauce. Crowd-pleaser comfort food.

What You'll Need

1 lb ground bison, 1 lb penne pasta, 1 can crushed tomatoes, 1 onion, 4 cloves garlic, 1 tsp each: basil, oregano, sugar, salt, parmesan, red pepper flakes

Brown bison, set aside. Sauté onion and garlic, add tomatoes and spices, simmer 20 min. Cook penne, reserve pasta water. Combine sauce and pasta, add pasta water to loosen. Finish with parmesan.

Pro Tips

Bison replaces beef in the classic — same comfort, less fat.
