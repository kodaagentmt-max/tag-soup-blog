Dutch Oven Sourdough Bread

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Crusty rustic sourdough baked in a Dutch oven over coals.

🫏 What You'll Need

2 cups sourdough starter, 4 cups flour, 1.5 tsp salt, 1 cup warm water

Mix into shaggy dough, let rise 4-6 hours. Shape into round, score top. Place in hot greased Dutch oven, cover. Stack 12 coals on bottom, 8 on top. Cook 30 min covered, 15 min uncovered until deep brown.

💡 Pro Tips

Requires a maintained sourdough starter — worth the effort for fresh camp bread.
