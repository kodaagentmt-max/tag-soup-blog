Venison Stir-Fry with Snap Peas and Ginger

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Venison loin sliced thin, seared fast with snap peas, ginger, and soy sauce.

🫏 What You'll Need

1 lb venison loin, 2 cups snap peas, 2 tbsp ginger, 3 cloves garlic, 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sesame oil, rice

Slice venison thin against the grain. Heat a cast iron or wok as hot as possible. Add oil, sear venison 1 minute per side, remove. Add snap peas, ginger, garlic — cook 2 minutes. Return venison, add soy sauce and sesame oil, toss and serve over rice.

💡 Pro Tips

Get the pan truly ripping hot. A cold pan = grey, tough meat. Do this fast.
