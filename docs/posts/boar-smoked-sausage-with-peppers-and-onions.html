Boar Smoked Sausage with Peppers and Onions

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Smoked wild boar sausage sliced and charred with sweet peppers and onions.

What You'll Need

1 lb smoked wild boar sausage, 2 bell peppers, 2 onions, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt, pepper

Slice sausage 1/2 inch thick, char in a hot cast iron. Set aside. Cook peppers and onions in the same pan until soft and slightly charred. Return sausage, season with paprika, salt, and pepper. Serve on its own or in a hoagie.

Pro Tips

The slightly gamey flavor of boar sausage is mellowed by the sweet peppers.
