Grouse Piccata with Capers and Lemon

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Pan-roasted grouse in a bright lemon-caper pan sauce.

🫏 What You'll Need

4 grouse breasts, 2 tbsp butter, 1/4 cup white wine, 1/4 cup chicken broth, 2 tbsp capers, 2 lemons, flour, salt, pepper

Dredge grouse breasts in flour, salt, pepper. Brown in butter in a hot pan 3 min per side. Remove. Add wine, broth, capers, and lemon juice to pan — reduce by half. Return grouse, baste with sauce, serve.

💡 Pro Tips

Grouse is tiny — portion one breast per person as an appetizer.
