Campfire Flatbread with Wild Boar Sausage

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Cast iron-grilled flatbread topped with sliced and charred wild boar sausage.

What You'll Need

2 cups flour, 1 cup warm water, 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp yeast, 1 lb wild boar sausage, 1 onion, mustard, cheese (optional)

Mix flour, water, salt, yeast — let rise 1 hour. Roll thin, grill on a cast iron over medium coals 2 min per side. Brown sausage in the same pan, char onion. Top flatbread with mustard, sausage, onion.

Pro Tips

The dough is simple — no rising time needed if you're in a hurry.
