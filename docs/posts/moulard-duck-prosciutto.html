Moulard Duck Prosciutto

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Duck breast air-cured for several weeks. Slice thin and serve raw.

What You'll Need

2 Moulard duck breasts, 1/4 cup coarse salt, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp black pepper, 1 tbsp thyme

Combine salt, sugar, pepper, thyme. Pack onto duck breasts, refrigerate 5-7 days, flipping daily. Rinse, pat dry, hang in a cool dry place (55°F) for 2-3 weeks. Slice paper-thin.

Pro Tips

This is a project that requires time and the right conditions — cool, dry basement or wine cellar.
