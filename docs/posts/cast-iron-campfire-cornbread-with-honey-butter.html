Cast Iron Campfire Cornbread with Honey Butter

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Golden cornbread with a crispy bacon-chili crust and honey butter.

🫏 What You'll Need

1.5 cups cornmeal, 1 cup flour, 1 tbsp baking powder, 1 tsp salt, 1 egg, 1 cup milk, 1/3 cup oil, 4 tbsp butter, 1/4 cup honey

Mix dry, whisk in egg, milk, and oil. Melt 2 tbsp butter in hot cast iron, pour in batter. Cook over medium coals 15 min, flip and cook 10 more min. Mix remaining butter with honey for serving.

💡 Pro Tips

The bacon drippings in the pan make the crust — don't substitute vegetable oil.
