Wild Boar

Wild Boar Bacon Smash Burger

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Smashed wild boar patties with crispy bacon, caramelized onions, and melted cheddar.

What You'll Need

1 lb ground wild boar, 4 strips bacon, 1 large onion, 4 brioche buns, 4 slices cheddar, salt, pepper, butter

Cook bacon until crisp, set aside. Slice onion thin, cook in bacon fat until deep golden. Form boar into 4 loose balls. Smash hard in a hot cast iron, season, cook 2 min, flip, add cheese and cover. Toast buns, assemble with bacon, onions.

Pro Tips

Smashing creates a crispy, lacey edge that's hard to beat.
