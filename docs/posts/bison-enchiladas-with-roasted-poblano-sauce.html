Bison

Bison Enchiladas with Roasted Poblano Sauce

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Rolled tortillas filled with bison and cheese, covered in smoky poblano sauce.

What You'll Need

1 lb ground bison, 12 corn tortillas, 4 poblano peppers, 2 cups shredded cheese, 1 onion, garlic, cumin, sour cream, chicken broth

Char poblanos, peel and blend with garlic, cumin, broth and a touch of sour cream. Brown bison with onion and garlic. Warm tortillas, fill with bison and cheese, roll and place in baking dish. Cover with poblano sauce and more cheese. Bake 375°F for 20 min.

Pro Tips

Poblanos range from mild to moderately hot — taste yours and adjust.
