Spoonbill Duck Breast with Plum Sauce

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Spoonbill duck breast seared rare with a quick plum sauce.

🫏 What You'll Need

2 spoonbill duck breasts, 1 cup plums, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar, 1 sprig rosemary, butter, salt, pepper

Score skin, salt and pepper. Sear skin-side down in a cold pan brought to medium heat, 12-15 min until crispy. Flip, cook 1 min. Rest. Add plums, sugar, vinegar, rosemary to pan — cook until plums burst and sauce thickens. Add butter, spoon over sliced duck.

💡 Pro Tips

Spoonbill has a mild flavor — the plum sauce is a natural partner.
