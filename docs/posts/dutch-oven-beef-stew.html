Dutch Oven Beef Stew

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Classic beef stew with root vegetables, browned meat, and rich gravy.

What You'll Need

2 lbs elk or bison shoulder, 4 potatoes, 3 carrots, 2 parsnips, 1 onion, 4 cups beef broth, tomato paste, thyme, bay leaf, flour, salt, pepper

Cut meat into chunks, brown in batches in Dutch oven. Add onion, cook 5 min. Dust with flour, stir. Add broth, tomato paste, herbs. Bring to simmer, cover and cook 1 hour. Add vegetables, cook 45 more min.

Pro Tips

A cast iron Dutch oven over coals is the best stew pot ever invented.
