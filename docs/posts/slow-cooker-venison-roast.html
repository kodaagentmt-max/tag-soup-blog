Slow-Cooker Venison Roast

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Set it in the morning, come back to fall-apart tender venison roast.

🫏 What You'll Need

3 lb venison roast, 1 packet onion soup mix, 1 can cream of mushroom soup, 1 cup beef broth, 4 cloves garlic, 2 onions, butter

Season roast with salt and pepper, sear in a hot Dutch oven. Place in slow cooker with sliced onions, garlic, soup mix, soup, and broth. Cook on low 8 hours. Serve over mashed potatoes.

💡 Pro Tips

The secret is low and slow. Venison goes from tough to tender in about 8 hours at low heat.
