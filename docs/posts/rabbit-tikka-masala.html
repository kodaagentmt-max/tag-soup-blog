Rabbit Tikka Masala

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Rabbit pieces in a rich, creamy tomato and yogurt masala sauce.

🫏 What You'll Need

1 whole rabbit (cut up), 1 cup yogurt, 3 tbsp garam masala, 1 can crushed tomatoes, 1 cup heavy cream, 1 onion, 4 cloves garlic, ginger, cumin, rice, cilantro

Marinate rabbit in yogurt, garam masala, and lemon 4 hours. Brown rabbit pieces, set aside. Sauté onion, garlic, ginger, cumin — add tomatoes, cream, and remaining spices. Return rabbit, simmer 45 min. Serve over basmati rice.

💡 Pro Tips

Rabbit takes the marinade beautifully — the yogurt tenderizes the lean meat.
