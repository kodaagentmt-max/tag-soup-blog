Muskie Fish Cakes with Cajun Remoulade

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Pan-fried muskellunge cakes with a spicy Cajun dipping sauce.

What You'll Need

1 lb muskellunge fillet (or pike), 2 eggs, 1/2 cup breadcrumbs, 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning, 1/2 cup mayo, 2 tbsp mustard, 1 tbsp horseradish, 1 tbsp pickle relish, lemon

Poach fish, cool and flake. Mix with eggs, breadcrumbs, and seasoning. Form into patties, pan-fry in oil until golden. Mix remoulade: mayo, mustard, horseradish, relish. Serve fish cakes with remoulade.

Pro Tips

Muskie is firm and mild — holds together well as a cake.
