Elk

Elk Meatballs in Tomato Basil Sauce

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Tender elk meatballs simmered in a simple tomato basil sauce. Lighter than beef, just as satisfying.

What You'll Need

1 lb ground elk, 1/4 cup breadcrumbs, 1 egg, 1/4 cup parmesan, 1 can crushed tomatoes, fresh basil, garlic, olive oil, salt, pepper

Mix elk with breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan, salt and pepper. Form into 12 balls. Brown in olive oil in a deep skillet, set aside. Add garlic to pan, cook 1 min, add tomatoes and basil, simmer 15 min. Return meatballs to sauce, cover and simmer 20 more minutes.

Pro Tips

Don't overmix the elk — mix just until combined or the meatballs will be tough.
