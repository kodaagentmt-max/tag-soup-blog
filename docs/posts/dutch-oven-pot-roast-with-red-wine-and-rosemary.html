Dutch Oven Pot Roast with Red Wine and Rosemary

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Elk or bison pot roast braised in red wine until fork-tender.

What You'll Need

3 lb elk or bison roast, 1 bottle red wine, 4 potatoes, 4 carrots, 1 onion, 4 cloves garlic, 3 sprigs rosemary, 2 tbsp butter, salt, pepper

Season and sear roast. Add sliced onion and garlic, cook 5 min. Pour in wine, add rosemary. Cover and cook over low coals 3 hours. Add potatoes and carrots in last hour.

Pro Tips

This works with any big-game roast — elk, bison, deer all benefit from the long braise.
