Elk Tacos with Roasted Salsa Verde

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Seasoned elk in warm corn tortillas with a bright tomatillo salsa verde.

🫏 What You'll Need

1 lb ground elk, corn tortillas, 1 lb tomatillos, 2 jalapenos, 1/2 onion, garlic, cilantro, cumin, lime, salt

Brown elk with cumin and salt, set aside. Char tomatillos, jalapenos, and onion under the broiler 5 minutes. Blend with garlic, cilantro, and lime. Warm tortillas, fill with elk and top with salsa verde.

💡 Pro Tips

Tomatillos can be swapped for green salsa from a jar — just doctor it with fresh lime and cilantro.
