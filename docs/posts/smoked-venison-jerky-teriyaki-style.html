Venison

Smoked Venison Jerky — Teriyaki Style

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Thin-sliced venison marinated in teriyaki, smoked low and slow until leathery.

🫏 What You'll Need

2 lbs venison, 1/2 cup soy sauce, 2 tbsp worcestershire, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp garlic powder, 1 tbsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp black pepper

Slice venison 1/4 inch thick against the grain. Mix marinade, combine with meat, marinate 12-24 hours in the fridge. Smoke at 130°F for 4-5 hours until dry and leathery but still flexible.

💡 Pro Tips

A food dehydrator works if you don't have a smoker. The key is getting the meat truly dry.
