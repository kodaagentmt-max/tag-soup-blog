Catfish Po' Boy with Remoulade

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Crispy catfish on a baguette with creamy remoulade and pickled vegetables.

🫏 What You'll Need

1 lb catfish fillets, 1 cup cornmeal, 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning, 1 baguette, 1/2 cup mayo, 2 tbsp Creole mustard, 1 tbsp pickle relish, shredded lettuce, tomato

Season catfish with Cajun spice, dredge in cornmeal, fry until golden. Mix mayo, mustard, relish for remoulade. Toast baguette, spread remoulade, pile on catfish, top with lettuce and tomato.

💡 Pro Tips

Catfish is mild and absorbs the spice beautifully — don't skip the remoulade.
