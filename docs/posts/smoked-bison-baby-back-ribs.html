Bison

Smoked Bison Baby Back Ribs

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Bison ribs slow-smoked until the meat pulls away from the bone.

🫏 What You'll Need

2 racks bison ribs, 1/4 cup brown sugar, 2 tbsp paprika, 1 tbsp each: garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, black pepper, salt, BBQ sauce

Remove membrane from back of ribs. Combine rub, apply generously. Smoke at 225°F for 5 hours. Wrap in foil with a splash of apple juice, cook 2 more hours. Unwrap, apply BBQ sauce, cook 30 more minutes.

💡 Pro Tips

They're done when you can pull a bone end and meat comes with it easily.
