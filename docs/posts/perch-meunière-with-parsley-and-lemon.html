Perch Meunière with Parsley and Lemon

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Classic French preparation: golden fried perch with brown butter, parsley, and lemon.

🫏 What You'll Need

1 lb perch fillets, 4 tbsp butter, 2 tbsp fresh parsley, 2 lemons, flour, salt, pepper

Dredge perch in flour with salt and pepper. Fry in butter in a cast iron until golden 2-3 min per side. Add more butter to the pan, cook until nutty brown. Pour over fish, squeeze lemon, scatter parsley.

💡 Pro Tips

Meunière is one of the simplest and best preparations for mild white fish.
