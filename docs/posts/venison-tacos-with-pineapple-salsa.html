Venison Tacos with Pineapple Salsa

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Seasoned venison in corn tortillas with fresh pineapple salsa and cilantro.

🫏 What You'll Need

1 lb ground venison, cumin, chili powder, salt, corn tortillas, 1 cup fresh pineapple, 1/4 cup red onion, cilantro, lime, jalapeno

Brown venison with cumin, chili powder, and salt. For salsa: dice pineapple, mix with minced red onion, cilantro, lime, and jalapeno. Warm tortillas, fill with venison, top with pineapple salsa.

💡 Pro Tips

The salsa can be made an hour ahead — the lime tenderizes the pineapple and develops flavor.
