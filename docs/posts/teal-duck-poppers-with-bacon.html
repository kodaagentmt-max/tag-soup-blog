Teal Duck Poppers with Bacon

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Spicy teal duck breast wrapped in bacon with a pepper jelly glaze.

What You'll Need

4 teal duck breasts, 8 slices bacon, 4 oz cream cheese, 2 tbsp jalapeno jelly, toothpicks

Slice duck breasts into 1-inch chunks. Wrap each in half-slice bacon, secure with toothpick. Season. Grill or smoke at 350°F for 20 min, brushing with jalapeno jelly in last 5 min. Serve with extra jelly for dipping.

Pro Tips

Any small duck works — teal are plentiful and the breast is just the right size.
