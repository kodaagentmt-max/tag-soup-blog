Venison

Venison Neck Ragu with Rigatoni

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Venison neck braised until it falls apart, served over thick rigatoni with parmesan.

What You'll Need

2 lbs venison neck, 1 can crushed tomatoes, 1 onion, 4 cloves garlic, 1 cup red wine, 1 lb rigatoni, parmesan, thyme, rosemary, salt, pepper

Season and brown neck pieces in Dutch oven. Add onion, garlic, cook 5 min. Add wine, reduce by half. Add tomatoes and herbs, cover and braise at 300°F for 3 hours until meat falls off bone. Shred meat, serve over rigatoni with parmesan.

Pro Tips

Neck is an underrated cut — cheap and incredible when braised low and slow.
