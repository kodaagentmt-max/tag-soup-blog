Canadian Goose Confit Legs

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Slow-cooked goose legs in duck fat until falling-off-the-bone tender.

What You'll Need

4 Canada goose legs, 2 cups duck fat or lard, 4 cloves garlic, thyme, salt, black pepper

Season goose legs, cover with duck fat in a Dutch oven. Cook at 325°F for 3 hours until very tender. Remove legs, reduce fat and reserve. Crisp legs skin-side down before serving. Serve with roasted root vegetables.

Pro Tips

Goose legs are tough without long cooking — the confit method transforms them.
