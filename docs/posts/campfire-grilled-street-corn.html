Campfire Grilled Street Corn

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Charred corn on the cob with mayo, cotija, chili, and lime.

What You'll Need

6 ears corn (in husk), 1/2 cup mayo, 1 cup cotija cheese (or feta), 2 tsp chili powder, 2 limes, cilantro, salt

Pull back husks, grill directly over hot coals until charred on all sides. Roll in mayo, sprinkle with cheese, chili powder, squeeze lime over top, scatter cilantro.

Pro Tips

Cotija is a crumbly Mexican cheese — feta is the closest substitute.
