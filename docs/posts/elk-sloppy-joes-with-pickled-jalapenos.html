Elk Sloppy Joes with Pickled Jalapenos

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Ground elk shoulder with pickled jalapenos, melted cheese on a toasted bun. Fast, filling, and ready in 20 minutes.

What You'll Need

1 lb ground elk, 4 hoagie buns, 1 cup pickled jalapenos, 4 slices American cheese, 1/2 cup BBQ sauce, 1 tbsp butter

Brown elk in a hot cast iron skillet, add BBQ sauce and jalapenos, simmer 5 minutes. Toast buns with butter, pile elk mixture on top, lay on cheese, serve immediately.

Pro Tips

Elk is lean — don't overcook or it'll dry out. The cheese melts faster if you cover the pan.
