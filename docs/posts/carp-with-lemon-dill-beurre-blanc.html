Carp with Lemon-Dill Beurre Blanc

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Pan-roasted carp with a silky butter sauce. A French bistro classic.

What You'll Need

1.5 lb carp fillet, 6 tbsp butter, 2 tbsp fresh dill, 2 shallots, 1/4 cup white wine, 1/4 cup heavy cream, lemon, salt, pepper

Season and sear carp skin-side down in 2 tbsp butter until crispy. Flip, cook through. Remove. Add shallots, wine, cream to pan — reduce by half. Whisk in remaining butter off heat, add dill and lemon. Spoon over carp.

Pro Tips

Beurre blanc is fragile — don't let it boil after you add the butter.
