Smoked Steelhead Trout Dip

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Creamy smoked trout dip with cream cheese, lemon, and fresh dill.

🫏 What You'll Need

8 oz smoked steelhead trout, 8 oz cream cheese, 1/4 cup sour cream, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tbsp fresh dill, 1 tbsp horseradish, salt, pepper

Flake trout into a bowl. Soften cream cheese, mix with sour cream, lemon, dill, horseradish. Fold in trout. Season and chill 1 hour. Serve with crackers.

💡 Pro Tips

Make it ahead — the flavors meld and improve overnight.
