Elk

Elk Chili Colorado

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Dried New Mexico chiles make a deep red sauce that transforms elk shoulder into something incredible.

What You'll Need

1.5 lbs elk shoulder, 4 dried New Mexico chiles, 1 can diced tomatoes, 1 onion, 3 cloves garlic, beef broth, cumin, salt

Toast chiles in dry pan 30 seconds, soak in hot water 20 min. Blend rehydrated chiles with some soaking liquid. Brown elk chunks, set aside. Sauté onion and garlic, add chile sauce, tomatoes, broth, return elk. Simmer covered 2.5 hours.

Pro Tips

New Mexico chiles are mild with a deep red color. They give the chili its signature flavor and color.
