Venison

Venison Salisbury Steak

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Ground venison patties in rich onion gravy. Old-school comfort food.

What You'll Need

1.5 lbs ground venison, 1 egg, 1/4 cup breadcrumbs, 1 onion, 2 cups beef broth, 2 tbsp flour, thyme, salt, pepper

Mix venison with egg, breadcrumbs, salt, pepper — form 6 patties. Brown in cast iron, set aside. Slice and sauté onion in the same pan. Add flour, stir 2 minutes, add broth and thyme, bring to simmer. Return patties, cover and cook 20 minutes.

Pro Tips

The gravy is built in the same pan — don't wash it between steps.
