Venison

Venison Bibimbap

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Korean-style rice bowl with seasoned venison, vegetables, and a fried egg.

What You'll Need

1 lb ground venison, 3 cups short-grain rice, 2 cups spinach, 1 cup bean sprouts, 1 zucchini, 2 carrots, 4 eggs, gochujang, sesame oil, soy sauce, garlic

Cook rice. Sauté vegetables separately with garlic and sesame oil. Brown venison with soy sauce. Arrange rice in bowls, top with vegetables and meat, fry eggs sunny-side up. Serve with gochujang.

Pro Tips

The key is getting each component cooked separately so flavors stay clean.
