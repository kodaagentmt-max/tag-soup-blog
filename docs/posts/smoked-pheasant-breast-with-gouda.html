Smoked Pheasant Breast with Gouda

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Smoked pheasant breast sliced thin over smoked Gouda and crackers.

What You'll Need

4 pheasant breasts, 4 oz smoked Gouda, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, apple wood chips

Cold-smoke or grill-smoke pheasant at 200°F for 1 hour. Let rest 10 min. Slice thin, fan out over Gouda slices on a board with crackers.

Pro Tips

Pheasant breasts are small — watch closely, they dry out fast.
