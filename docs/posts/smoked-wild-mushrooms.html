Smoked Wild Mushrooms

[*] Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Forest mushrooms slow-smoked with hickory until deeply savory.

What You'll Need

2 lbs mixed mushrooms, 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar, 2 cloves garlic, hickory chips, salt

Clean and halve mushrooms. Toss with oil, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and salt. Smoke at 180°F for 2 hours until leathery and dark brown. Serve warm or cold as a side dish.

Pro Tips

Smoked mushrooms are umami bombs — chop and add to pasta, risotto, or eggs.
