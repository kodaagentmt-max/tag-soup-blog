Elk

Elk Green Chile Stew

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Tender elk shoulder with roasted green chiles, onion, and garlic. One pot, deeply satisfying.

🫏 What You'll Need

1.5 lbs elk shoulder, 4 roasted green chiles, 1 onion, 3 cloves garlic, 2 cups beef broth, cumin, salt, pepper, lime

Cut elk into 1-inch chunks, brown in Dutch oven. Add diced onion and garlic, cook 5 min. Add chiles, broth, cumin, bring to boil, reduce to low, cover and simmer 2 hours. Season and squeeze lime over it.

💡 Pro Tips

Elk shoulder is best for this — the long braise breaks down the tissue. Use canned green chiles in a pinch.
