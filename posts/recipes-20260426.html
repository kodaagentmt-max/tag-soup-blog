Dutch Oven Campfire Chili — 5 Hours, Worth the Wait

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup • April 26, 2026

Five hours over coals transforms tough elk shoulder into something that falls apart at the touch of a fork. This dutch oven campfire chili is the ultimate field-to-table meal. Brown the meat hard, layer in the beans and tomatoes, and let the fire do the rest. The bone-deep warmth and rich gamey flavor make this the definitive camp meal for cold nights.
