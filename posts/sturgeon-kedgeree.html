Sturgeon Kedgeree

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Smoked sturgeon flaked into curried rice with hard-boiled eggs. British colonial comfort.

🫏 What You'll Need

1 lb smoked sturgeon (or salmon), 3 cups cooked rice, 4 eggs, 1 onion, 1 tsp curry powder, 1/4 cup cream, butter, lemon, parsley

Hard-boil eggs, set aside. Sauté onion in butter, add curry powder, cook 1 min. Add rice and cream, heat through. Flake in sturgeon, fold in eggs, season, add lemon and parsley.

💡 Pro Tips

Smoked sturgeon is worth seeking out — the firm flesh is extraordinary.
