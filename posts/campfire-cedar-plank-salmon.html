Campfire Cedar Plank Salmon

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Salmon slow-roasted on a cedar plank over the campfire. Infinitely smoky.

🫏 What You'll Need

2 lb salmon fillet, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 lemon, 4 sprigs dill, 2 cloves garlic, salt, pepper

Soak cedar plank 1 hour. Oil plank, lay salmon skin-side down. Top with lemon slices, dill, garlic. Place near (not in) hot coals for 45-60 min until fish flakes easily. Don't flip.

💡 Pro Tips

Keep the fire hot and the plank away from direct flame — you want smoking heat, not cooking heat.
