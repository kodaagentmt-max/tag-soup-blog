Bison Burgers with Caramelized Onions and Gruyère

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Juicy bison burgers topped with slow-cooked onions and melted Gruyère.

🫏 What You'll Need

1.5 lbs ground bison, 2 large onions, 4 slices Gruyère, 4 brioche buns, butter, salt, pepper

Slice onions thin, cook slowly in butter 25 minutes until deep golden. Season bison with salt and pepper, form 4 patties. Cook in cast iron 3-4 min per side for medium. Add cheese, cover to melt. Toast buns, assemble.

💡 Pro Tips

Keep bison burgers medium-rare to medium — any more and they dry out.
