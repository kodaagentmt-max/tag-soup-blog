Elk

Elk Rueben Sandwich

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Thin-sliced elk roast on rye with caramelized sauerkraut, Swiss cheese, and Russian dressing.

🫏 What You'll Need

1 lb elk deli slices or thin-sliced roast, 2 cups sauerkraut, 4 slices rye bread, 4 slices Swiss cheese, Russian dressing, butter

Caramelize sauerkraut in a buttered pan 5 minutes. Toast rye with butter until golden. Layer elk, sauerkraut, cheese, and dressing on one slice, top with another, press on the griddle until cheese melts.

💡 Pro Tips

If using a whole roast instead of deli slices, sear it hard then roast at 325°F until medium-rare internally.
