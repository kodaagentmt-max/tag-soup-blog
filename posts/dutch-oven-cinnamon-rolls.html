Dutch Oven Cinnamon Rolls

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Gooey cinnamon rolls with cream cheese frosting baked in a Dutch oven.

🫏 What You'll Need

2 tubes refrigerator biscuits, 4 tbsp butter, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 2 tbsp cinnamon, 4 oz cream cheese, 1/4 cup powdered sugar, 2 tbsp milk

Melt butter in Dutch oven. Mix brown sugar and cinnamon. Separate biscuits, dip in butter, coat with sugar mixture. Arrange in pot. Cook over medium coals 15 min until golden. Mix cream cheese, powdered sugar, milk — frost warm rolls.

💡 Pro Tips

Refrigerator biscuits are the camp-friendly shortcut — nobody needs to know.
