Walleye Au Gratin

🔥 Camp Cook Kitchen — Tag Soup

Walleye fillets baked under a golden cracker crust with cheese and cream.

🫏 What You'll Need

1.5 lbs walleye fillets, 1 cup crushed saltines, 1 cup shredded cheddar, 1 cup heavy cream, 3 tbsp butter, 1 shallot, salt, pepper, paprika

Place walleye in a buttered baking dish. Top with cream and minced shallot. Cover with crushed crackers and cheddar, dot with butter. Bake 375°F for 25 min until golden and bubbling.

💡 Pro Tips

Heavy cream makes this luxuriously rich — a little goes a long way.
